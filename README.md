\# Simulated Fruit Fly Brain Plays Video Games



PC Gamer: \[After Google mapped an adult male fruit fly's brain, software engineers made it play Doom, Mario64, and Beat Saber](https://www.pcgamer.com/hardware/after-google-mapped-an-adult-male-fruit-flys-brain-software-engineers-made-it-play-doom-mario64-and-beat-saber/)



\## Why I Found This Interesting



This article shows an amazing crossing of worlds between neuroscience and software engineering. Google Researchers mapped over 166,000 neurons of an adult male fruit fly's central nervous system, creating a digital connectome. Almost immediately, developers wired inputs from games like \*Doom\* and \*Super Mario 64\* directly into the simulated fly's sensory neurons, using dopamine triggers for reinforcement learning.



What made this cool to me is how we can make digital biology. Instead of programming traditional artificial intelligence, developers are using virtual organism biology to process visual inputs and trigger game actions. It offers a unique glimpse into how digital neural maps might change AI development and computational biology in the future.
